Formats we handle

A vial and a tablet strip are not the same problem.

Each format brings its own handling risk. These are the six we move most, and what each one demands.

Vials & ampoules

Glass primary packaging, most often cold chain. Handled with breakage protection and upright orientation through picking and despatch.

Breakage & temperature risk

Pre-filled syringes

Device-and-drug combinations that fail silently if frozen. Conditioned coolants are used rather than direct-contact ice packs.

Freeze-sensitive

Lyophilised powders

Freeze-dried injectables, common in oncology and critical care. Stable at ambient in many cases, but seal integrity is checked at goods-in.

Seal integrity checked

Tablets & capsules

Blister and bottle formats across chronic and general medicine. Picked to batch, with near-expiry stock flagged before it reaches an order.

Near-expiry flagged

Topicals & creams

Tubes and jars for dermatology and wound care. Kept away from heat sources in storage and shipped in ambient-controlled configurations.

Heat-sensitive

Devices & diagnostic kits

Ostomy and wound care, surgical consumables and IVD kits. Some components carry their own storage requirement independent of the box.

Component-level storage

Buyer questions

Before you place the first order.

01 Is there a minimum order quantity?

For institutional buyers, no fixed minimum — order sizes follow the hospital's own consumption pattern. For named-patient oncology supply, quantities are set by the prescription rather than by us. Bulk and palletised lines such as sanitisation do carry practical minimums, which we state at the point of enquiry.

02 What expiry dating do you supply against?

Batch and expiry are confirmed at the point of availability, before you commit to the order. Near-expiry stock is flagged in our system and is never substituted into an order silently — if the only stock available is short-dated, we tell you and let you decide.

03 How do you handle cold chain products to non-metro locations?

The packing configuration is chosen against the specific lane and expected transit duration, not applied as a single standard. Longer or less predictable lanes get more conditioned coolant and, for the products that warrant it, a data logger. The temperature record is reviewed on arrival.

04 What is the returns and recall process?

Returns are accepted per the principal's own policy and processed against the original batch. In a recall, batch-level traceability from goods-in to proof of delivery lets us identify exactly which consignments went where, and to whom, without reconstructing it from paperwork.

05 Can you supply directly to a patient?

Yes, for anti-cancer and speciality medicines. Supply is against a verified prescription and coordinated with the treating hospital, with the cold chain maintained to the door. This is a significant part of what we do rather than an exception we accommodate.

06 Do you share a full brand and SKU list?

A complete list of brands and pack sizes across all 23 principals is issued to registered institutional buyers on request. It changes often enough that publishing a static version on the website would mislead more than it helps.
