Who we are

A warehouse business that behaves like a clinical partner.

Mediline Biotex Pvt. Ltd. is one of Bangalore's major pharmaceutical supply chain warehouses. We hold stock for Indian majors, multinationals and speciality innovators, and release it to hospitals, pharmacies and — where the therapy demands it — directly to the patient.

Three branches — two in Bangalore, on Sarjapur Main Road and Hosa Road, and one in Mangalore — sit behind multiple warehouses covering distribution, CFA and CSA operations, retail supply and named-patient dispatch.

  • Batch and expiry controlled from goods-in to proof of delivery
  • Packing configurations matched to lane, season and transit time
  • Client SOPs followed as written — not adapted to ours
  • Pan-India delivery through established logistics partners

Nisha

Nisha founded Mediline Biotex Pvt. Ltd. and heads the three businesses at its core: carrying & forwarding operations, CSA operations and corporate hospital supplies. She oversees how principals’ stock is received, held and released under each CFA and CSA mandate, and how it reaches the corporate hospital groups the company serves. Asked by procurement

The five questions we get before every appointment.

Do you hold the drug licences for our territory?

Yes. Mediline Biotex operates under wholesale drug licences issued by the Karnataka Drugs Control Department, and licence copies are shared as part of onboarding rather than on request. For territories outside Karnataka we work through the appropriate registrations for that state.
