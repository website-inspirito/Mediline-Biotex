Before you write

Questions we can answer here.

01 How quickly will I get a reply?

Same working day for stock enquiries on lines we hold. The reply gives availability, batch, expiry and a delivery window rather than a simple yes or no, so you can plan against a date. CFA and partnership enquiries usually get an initial response the same day and a substantive one within a few days, because they need a capacity check first.

02 Do you supply outside Karnataka?

Yes. Deliveries reach metro and non-metro cities nationwide through established logistics partners, with packing configured to the specific lane and expected transit time. Our carrying and forwarding mandates cover Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and all-India central warehousing depending on the principal.

03 Can I visit the warehouse before appointing you?

We would prefer it. Site visits for prospective principals are arranged on request at the Sarjapur Main Road head office — the cold room, the racking, the segregation and the records behind them. Contact the director's line to arrange one.

04 What documentation do you provide at onboarding?

Wholesale drug licences, the ISO 9001:2015 certificate, the WHO-GDP certificate, and company registration details. Both certifications can be verified independently at the issuing bodies — qrocert.org and ukascert.co.uk.

05 Do you supply directly to patients?

Yes, for anti-cancer and speciality medicines, against a verified prescription and coordinated with the treating hospital, with the cold chain maintained to the door. Write to the operations desk with the prescription details and the treating hospital.
