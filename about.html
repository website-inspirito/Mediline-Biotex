Who we are

A warehouse business that behaves like a clinical partner.

Most of what a distributor does is invisible when it goes right. A consignment leaves a manufacturer, sits on a rack for a while, and arrives at a hospital pharmacy intact, on the date somebody promised. Nobody notices.

It gets noticed when it fails — when a batch cannot be traced, when a cold chain product arrives warm, when an oncology patient is told their cycle is postponed a week. We built this company around the parts that fail.

Three branches — two in Bangalore, on Sarjapur Main Road and Hosa Road, and one in Mangalore — sit behind multiple warehouses covering distribution, CFA and CSA operations, super distribution, retail supply and named-patient dispatch.

  • ISO 9001:2015 and WHO Good Distribution Practice certified
  • Batch and expiry controlled from goods-in to proof of delivery
  • Packing configurations matched to lane, season and transit time
  • Client SOPs followed as written — not adapted to ours

What we run

Five operations, one stock position.

Pharmaceutical companies use us as a state or national CFA, or as a super distributor. Hospitals buy from us directly. All of it sits on the same racks, the same system and the same team.

01 / Distribution

Stockist & wholesale

Generic, branded and speciality medicines supplied to hospital pharmacies, chemists and institutional buyers across metro and non-metro cities.

02 / CFA & CSA

Carrying & forwarding

Five active mandates, ₹44.99 Cr of principal stock handled in 2025–26. We hold the stock, invoice on the principal's behalf and report back on movement, ageing and returns.

03 / Super distribution

Appointed super stockist

Three principals route their regional stock through us for onward distribution to sub-stockists and retail.

04 / Cold chain

Temperature-controlled

Cold, cool and ambient storage with packing configurations matched to transit time and lane rather than applied uniformly.

05 / Patient supply

Direct to patient

Named-patient supply of anti-cancer and speciality medicines, dispatched against prescription and coordinated with the treating hospital.
