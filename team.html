Working with us

What a principal can expect from the team.

The commitments below are not aspirational. They are what the ISO 9001 quality system requires us to evidence at each surveillance audit.

  • A named operations lead, introduced at onboarding, who stays with the account
  • Secondary sales reporting delivered on your cycle, not ours
  • Near-expiry stock flagged before it reaches an order, never substituted silently
  • Temperature excursions reported, not absorbed to protect a service metric
  • A site visit before you commit, and whenever you want one afterwards
